Branch 1 - Marketing: create demand

B2B

Buyer is a committee - multi-thread economic buyer, champion, blocker. Signals before sends. Cold and content carry; paid runs last. Root metric: cost per booked call.

› 1.1Targeting & List - who we hit % of list matching ICP · bounce < 2%
1.1.1 ICP definition
Firmographics, tech stack, trigger criteria.
1.1.2 TAM mapping
Tier A/B/C accounts, account scoring. The B2B “ABM” base.
1.1.3 List building
Software → brokers → hand-build the first 1,000. List quality beats clever copy.
1.1.4 Data quality
Dedupe, enrich, verify emails, validate before any send.
1.1.5 Signal layer
Funding, hiring, tech install, leadership change, filings. No send without a reason.
1.1.6 Deliverability infra
Domains, DMARC, warmup, spam-placement checks.
› 1.2Message & Offer - what we say engaged reply rate · cold email benchmark ~3%
1.2.1 Positioning
Outcome, mechanism, who it’s for, why now.
1.2.2 Hook bank
20+ per piece. Families: outcome, time, effort reduction, callout, proof, contrarian, mechanism.
1.2.3 Marketing offer
Lead-magnet promise vs core-offer promise. Name the outcome, not the format.
1.2.4 Proof assets
One lookalike case study beats ten generic testimonials.
1.2.5 Objection pre-emption
Top 3-5 objections answered inside the copy, before the call.
› 1.3Demand Generation - the Core Four, subdivided engaged leads per channel · cost per engaged lead
1.3.1 Warm outreach (warm × 1:1)
100/day · A-C-A · “know anybody” primer · ≤3 follows · ~1 customer per 100 reach-outs.
1.3.2 Cold outreach (cold × 1:1)
Email 100-200/day · DM 30-50/day · calls 50-100 dials. 3-5 sentences, value in message 1, no ask. 5-7 touches; replies land on 3-5.
1.3.3 Content (warm × 1:many)
1 long-form/week → 7+ derivatives → daily posts. Give:ask ≥ 3:1. “How I” not “how to.” LinkedIn, newsletter, YouTube, events.
1.3.4 Paid ads (cold × 1:many)
Run last. LinkedIn (title/account targeting), Google (intent), retargeting. 5-10 hooks per concept. Kill at 2× target CPL. Scale +20%/day max.
1.3.5 Lead Getters
Referrals ≥ 25% of new business, follow up <24h · employees · agencies with a deadline · affiliates paid 25 / 50 / 100% of max CAC at agree / activate / sustain.
› 1.4Capture - how they raise a hand opt-in rate · magnet ≈ 10× engaged leads at ≈ 3× lower CAC
1.4.1 Lead magnets
3 types (reveal the problem, sample/trial, one step of a multi-step) × 4 deliveries (software, info, service, physical). Narrow problem, one sitting, charge-worthy.
1.4.2 Landing page
Continues the ad’s exact promise. Mismatch kills conversion.
1.4.3 CTA
One action + real scarcity, urgency, or bonus. Never stack CTAs.
1.4.4 Qualification
Form fields, routing rules, disqualify fast.
› 1.5Nurture & Handoff - after the hand raise cost per booked call
1.5.1 Speed-to-lead
First response < 5 min. Named owner. Alert fires. Response rates fall ~80% after 30 min.
1.5.2 Sequence
Day 0/1/2/3/5/7/10/14. Value in every touch. No “just checking in.”
1.5.3 Retargeting
Ads pointed at engaged non-bookers.
1.5.4 List warming
9-word email to dormant list. Re-run every 3-6 months with a new angle.
1.5.5 Handoff contract
MQL definition, routing to setter, booked-call SLA.
› 1.6Ops & Measurement - the plumbing LTGP:CAC per channel · nothing scales below 3:1
1.6.1 Tracking
Pixel, UTMs, per-channel attribution.
1.6.2 Dashboard
CPL, cost per booked call, CAC, LTGP:CAC per channel.
1.6.3 Testing discipline
One test/week on the biggest drop-off. Abandon after 4 tries or 1 month.
1.6.4 Automation
Enrichment, sequencing, routing, reporting. The GTM-engineer layer.
